01Introduction

Overview

EV Energy & Driving Mode Dashboard is a UXUI Concept design for In-Vehicle Infotainment systems on electric vehicles. This design is inspired by the car BYD SEALION 6.

02User research

EV HMI Driving Mode Personas

Understanding distinct user expectations across Efficiency, Comfort, and Performance cohorts to design intuitive, customizable electric vehicle infotainment systems.

Minh - The Range Optimizer

Researcher & Sustainability Advocate

AGE 34 ECO MODE

Vehicle

VF5

Behavioral Bio

Minh treats range maximization like a puzzle to solve. She analyzes her daily energy consumption stats to shave off watt-hours, relying heavily on regenerative braking feedback and real-time battery health diagnostics.

Goals

  • Maximize real-world vehicle range
  • Monitor long-term battery degradation
  • Automate smart off-peak charging schedules

Frustrations

  • Unpredictable Range Estimate (GOM)
  • Lack of granular historic consumption data
  • Unclear power-draw feedback from climate control
  • Eco mode reduces the air conditioner's power, causing discomfort in hot weather without any clear notification.

Key HMI Metrics Required

SOC % & kWh Regen Braking Efficiency Consumption Trend (Wh/km)

Marcus - Practical & Simple

Product Manager & Family Man

AGE 41 NORMAL MODE

Vehicle

Tesla Model Y

Behavioral Bio

Marcus wants his EV to blend seamlessly into his family routine. He values a balanced, predictable driving feel, instant access to map/audio controls, and clear, non-intrusive safety notifications that keep his family safe.

Goals

  • Dependable, fuss-free daily commuting range
  • Quick access to climate, navigation & audio
  • Clear, high-priority safety & ADAS alerts

Frustrations

  • Information overload with complex menus
  • Too many taps required for common adjustments
  • Laggy or non-responsive touchscreen buttons

Key HMI Metrics Required

Distance to Empty HVAC Status & Audio Info Active Safety Alerts

Mina - Outdoor Adventurer

Engineer & Track Driver

AGE 28 SPORT MODE

Vehicle

Porsche Macan Electric

Behavioral Bio

Mina expects her EV to be a precision instrument. She seeks dynamic telemetry, immediate throttle feedback, and high-fidelity power output visualizations. To her, a dashboard is a performance tuning command center.

Goals

  • Monitor real-time torque & thermal load
  • Record and analyze track lap performance
  • Customize power distribution and suspension stiffness

Frustrations

  • Laggy, low-refresh instrumentation
  • Overly-filtered, sterile driving feel on dashboard
  • Inability to disable aggressive stability aids

Key HMI Metrics Required

Instant kW Output & G-Force Motor & Battery Thermals Lap Timer & Tire Pressure

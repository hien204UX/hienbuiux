About

User-centered with attention to detail

Hien Bui — Ho Chi Minh City, 2024

I grew up in Ho Chi Minh City and studied communication design before moving into product. Early in my career I was a UI/UX designer for FPT Software Malaysia for 5 years, working with the DesignOps and Product team. There I learned to approach work with a strong philosophy of ethics-first decision-making.

Now I work with product teams in the UAE to bring the same rigour to fintech digital products. I care about the micro-states and the moment a real person first opens the thing we made.
